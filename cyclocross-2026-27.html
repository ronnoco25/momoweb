Notes and caveats

  • US streaming is expected, not confirmed per race. FloBikes carried the UCI World Cup and the 2026 World Championships in the US, but did not show every World Cup round in 2025-26. HBO Max (Standard plan or higher, $18.49 a month in 2025-26) showed a number of Superprestige, X2O Trofee and Exact Cross (now HG Cross) races in 2025-26. I found no 2026-27 US announcement, so check the FloBikes schedule and HBO Max listings before each race. The UCI YouTube World Cup stream is sometimes available in the US.
  • Start times. Only Overijse (Oct 25) has one: 13:50 local, from its Cyclocross24 page, which does not say which category. The Eastern time is my conversion.
  • Cyclocross24 links. "Race page" opens that race. Where I could not confirm a race page, the link opens the series page that lists every round. Race pages show the latest edition on file, so some may still show 2025-26 dates until the new edition is added (Loenhout showed Dec 29, 2025 when I looked).
  • Left out. European Championships (no 2026 date found), national championships, and US races. FloBikes event pages for Exact Cross Kortrijk (Dec 13) and X2O Loenhout (Dec 29) conflict with organizer calendars, which list Loenhout on Dec 23 and no Kortrijk round.
  • Sources. Cyclingnews (World Cup calendar, US streaming guide), Flanders Classics and CyclingFlash (Superprestige), x2otrofee.be and Veldritkrant (X2O Trofee), hgcross.be (HG Cross), ProCyclingUK and RideCX (2025-26 US coverage), and Cyclocross24 (race pages and Overijse time).
